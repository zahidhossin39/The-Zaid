5. **Scores for all nine directions** (3 per critic, including your own) out of 10, each with a one-line reason.
6. **Your three final directions**, revised using everything above. Each must be structurally different from the other two, fully specced (px/rem, grid numbers, card spec, type sizes), and predicted to score **at least 9.0/10**. For each, list which merged flaws it fixes and what it trades away. If a direction can't reach 9.0, replace it.
