# Research + ideation task: make one film feel "made for me" to four different niches

You are one of three independent researchers (Claude, Gemini, Kimi) working on the same question. Work alone; later you will critique the others. Research AND reason: find what others actually do, then invent what we should do better. Research only: do NOT create, edit or delete any files. Output markdown to stdout. No em dashes.

## The business
Zaid Hossain, a solo builder with no clients yet. He sells growth outcomes (fuller calendar, time back) to small appointment businesses, built from: a voice agent that answers every call and books; instant replies that book on Instagram DM, SMS/iMessage, Messenger, WhatsApp, web chat; no-show reminders and waitlist refill of cancelled slots; win-back of lapsed clients; review requests; booking websites. Target niches: (1) dental and medical practices, (2) gyms, yoga and fitness studios, (3) personal trainers and coaches, (4) med spas. Market: US first, then UK, Canada, Australia. Website goal: book a free 20-min call.

## The film
A ~45 s muted-autoplay motion-graphics film (code-built UI, no faces or real people, never the word "AI") for his site's services section and social. Current story, "Same afternoon, twice": the owner is busy with a client; their phone lights up on the counter (a call, an Instagram DM "tooth is killing me", a "can't make 4pm" cancellation). Beside it we see the client's phone: nobody answers, they book a competitor. At 2:46 the owner sends "sorry just seeing this!!" and gets "all good, found someone". Rewind, "Same afternoon. Take two.": every call and message is now answered and booked, the cancelled slot is refilled from a waitlist. The owner picks up the phone to "3 booked, 1 slot refilled". The film ends on a full week with one evening off.

## The problem to solve
If the film is set in a dental practice, a gym owner thinks "not for me". Niche tabs ("Dental / Fitness / Med spa / Trainer") above the video were rejected: visitors should not have to choose or click anything. It must feel general enough that no one feels excluded, yet specific enough that each owner instantly thinks "that's MY business", with zero effort from the viewer, and stay easy to understand.

## What to deliver
1. **What others do (research, cite URLs).** How do companies and agencies that serve many verticals make each visitor feel "this is for me" without making them pick: e.g. Podium, Weave, Birdeye, Square, Mindbody, Jobber, Housecall Pro, Calendly, GoHighLevel agencies, Vagaro, Boulevard, Toast, Shopify, Stripe, HubSpot, Intercom, plus ad and film examples (montages of many customers, rotating words, universal objects, personalised landing pages from ad/UTM, dynamic text). Note what works, what is generic or overused, and any evidence (A/B tests, CRO studies) on personalisation and self-identification.
2. **At least 12 concrete ideas** for OUR film and site. Include safe, clever and wild ones. For each: how it works on screen, second by second where relevant; why each of the 4 niches recognises itself; its flaws; how to fix the flaws.
3. **Score each idea out of 10** on this rubric, and give the weighted total:
   - Instant self-recognition (25%)
   - Zero effort for the viewer (20%)
   - Clarity and simplicity when muted (20%)
   - Craft and wow (15%)
   - Solo buildability in HTML/GSAP plus an Astro site (10%)
   - Scales to new niches later (10%)
4. **Your top 3**, improved until each would score 9 or higher. Explain what you changed to get there.
