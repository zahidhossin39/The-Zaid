## A. Owners' own words

### Lead follow-up and DMs
- Two-Brain says the average gym gets about **"30 to 35 leads a month"**, and the problem is keeping them, not getting them. It cites HBR: businesses were **"60 times more likely to qualify a lead when they responded within the first hour versus waiting a full day."** (https://twobrainbusiness.com/5-dm-mistakes/)
- Two-Brain lead-nurture article: **"14.4 percent of gyms never call their leads at all"**, and fewer than 1% call within the first hour. (https://twobrainbusiness.com/6-simple-lead-nurture-strategies/)
- Show-rate leaderboard (Oct 2021): the best gyms' consult show rates ranged **24%–57%**. Tactics from top owners:
  - one sends **"reminders of appointments 48 hours in advance—both email and text, followed by a text one hour before"**
  - another says **"text new leads right away: 'Here is our website, here is helpful content'"**
  - (https://twobrainbusiness.com/show-rate-october-2021/)
  - What it means: even the best gyms lose roughly half their booked consults. Show rate is a direct lever Zaid can pull.

### Software pain (what owners pay for and still hate)

  - "customer service is nearly impossible to reach. When you finally get through, instead of speaking to a real human you get AI responses"
  - "My subscription has climbed to over $1,000 CAD/month."
  - "terribly over priced and very difficult to use for both the business and the clients"
  - "I was offered a free trial and was stuck in a one year contract"
  - "High cost for more features. I have stayed at the lowest tier and made due with the features"

  - "The system cant even send out an email for a cancelled class's. The only option to notify clientele is if they 1. have the app downloaded & 2 have push notifications turned on" (a gap Zaid can fill with an SMS workflow)
  - "Amendments to memberships are cumbersome and require alot of manual input."
  - "one of my clients purchased a package from the app for which they ended up with FOUR packages charged FOUR times to their credit card"
  - "integrations constantly glitch."
  - "Limited integration with MailChimp."
- Wodify on Capterra: an owner said the platform's faults "cost them sales" and called it "jack of all trades; master of none". This is a search-result paraphrase, so verify it before quoting. (https://www.capterra.com/compare/159663-172781/Wodify-vs-PushPress)
- Mindbody on Trustpilot: studio customers report being locked into contracts, being charged "an extra month's payment on a technicality", and a new card being charged after they cancelled. Paraphrased from the search summary. (https://www.trustpilot.com/review/mindbodyonline.com?page=9, BBB: https://www.bbb.org/us/ca/san-luis-obispo/profile/computer-software-developers/mindbody-inc-1236-5002899/complaints)

**Takeaway:** owners already pay $150–$1,000+/mo for software and still do follow-up, messaging and membership edits by hand. Pitch the missing layer on top of their tool (speed-to-lead, SMS, reminders). Don't pitch a replacement.

## B. Member side (what members complain about)
Planet Fitness on Trustpilot (https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.planetfitness.com):
- "I cancelled my Planet Fitness membership and I have PROOF that it was cancelled. Despite that, my card was still charged OVER $300"
- "The telephone number for this location does not accept voicemail, and more than five emails have gone unanswered."
- "They make it very difficult for you to cancel your membership. Deactivated account sent letter like instructed than nothing except a new charge"
- "I was left dealing with continued charges and the possibility of having an account sent to collections."
- "...has no difficulty collecting membership payments every month, but I have been unable to reliably use the facility and cannot reach anyone to discuss the problem."

Planet Fitness cancellation needs an in-person visit or a certified letter at least 7 days before billing (https://legalclarity.org/how-to-cancel-planet-fitness-in-person-mail-or-online-4/).

The same themes show up in Google reviews of independent gyms and studios: calls that go nowhere, DMs left on read, and billing after cancelling. This is my synthesis, and I haven't counted it. **For Zaid:** a phone that always answers, instant DM/SMS replies, and a clean self-serve cancel or pause flow all protect the star rating.

## 2. Day in the life

**Roles by size**
- **Solo-owner studio (yoga/pilates/boutique, 1 room):** the owner is also lead instructor, marketer, front desk and bookkeeper. Teaches 10–20 classes a week and answers DMs between classes. No front desk.
- **Established single gym/box (200–800 members):**
  - owner/GM
  - 3–15 coaches or instructors (often part-time 1099s)
  - maybe a part-time front desk or "client success manager"
  - often a sales person who runs "No Sweat Intros" (Two-Brain's free intro consult)
  - HVLC (high-volume, low-cost) 24/7 gyms run unstaffed hours with key-fob access
- **Multi-location / franchise:**
  - regional manager plus a GM per site
  - a sales team with quotas
  - a centralised call centre or none at all, which is where "no one answers" reviews come from
  - HQ owns marketing and software (ABC, ClubReady, Mindbody Enterprise)

**Daily rhythm**
- 5–9am: peak classes. The owner or coach is on the floor, so phones and DMs go unanswered.
- 9am–4pm: admin window.
  - reply to overnight leads and DMs, which are hours old by now
  - chase failed payments
  - edit memberships (freezes, upgrades)
  - find subs for sick instructors
  - post on Instagram
  - intro consults
- 4–8pm: second peak. Again nobody is at the phone.
- Evening: the owner answers DMs from bed.

**Weekly**
- billing run and a failed-payments list
- instructor schedule and sub gaps
- check attendance for members going quiet (missing 7–14 days)
- ask for reviews
- ad spend check (FB/IG lead ads through a GHL agency or Gym Lead Machine)

**Monthly:** churn and retention numbers, and challenge or promo launches.

**Software and integrations**
- **CrossFit/functional gyms:** PushPress, Wodify, Zen Planner.
- **Yoga/pilates/boutique:**
  - Mindbody
  - ABC Glofox
  - Arketa, Momence, Walla, Marianatek
  - TeamUp (UK/EU)
- **Big-box / HVLC:** ABC Evo, ClubReady.
- **Gyms in general:** Gymdesk.
- **Integration notes:**
  - Mindbody has a public API (paid developer access, per-site activation fee).
  - PushPress and Glofox have APIs or Zapier and webhooks.
  - Many small gyms bolt GoHighLevel (GHL) on as the CRM and SMS layer through a "gym agency".
- **The gap:** the booking tool holds the members, the CRM or GHL holds the leads, and nothing connects them to the phone line. That junction is Zaid's wedge.

## 3. Ranked pain points

### 1. Slow or no lead follow-up (IG/FB ads, DMs, web forms, missed calls)
- **What happens:** leads arrive while the owner is coaching. The reply comes hours later and the lead goes cold.
- **Who suffers:** owner or sales staff, and ad spend.
- **Cost:**
  - 30–35 leads/mo × a 60x qualification drop after the first hour
  - gym LTV is often $1.5k–$3k+ (Two-Brain-style boxes charge $150–250/mo; **[estimate]**)
  - a few lost members a month = $5k+/yr
- **Evidence:** "60 times more likely to qualify a lead when they responded within the first hour" and "14.4 percent of gyms never call their leads at all" (https://twobrainbusiness.com/5-dm-mistakes/, https://twobrainbusiness.com/6-simple-lead-nurture-strategies/)
- **Fix:**
  - instant chat/DM and SMS reply that books the intro
  - AI voice agent for missed calls
  - **Ease:** medium. The IG/Meta API and GHL are easy; writing bookings into Mindbody/PushPress is medium.

### 2. Intro/trial no-shows and low trial-to-member conversion
- **What happens:** booked intros don't show, and trials fade out without an offer.
- **Who suffers:** coaches who sit idle, and revenue.
- **Cost:** top gyms still only hit a 24–57% show rate. Every no-show wastes the lead cost and a coach slot.
- **Evidence:** the show-rate leaderboard, where the fix is 48h email+text plus a 1h text reminder (https://twobrainbusiness.com/show-rate-october-2021/)
- **Fix:**
  - reminder sequence plus a personal video or text
  - trial-day nudges and an offer on the last day
  - **Ease:** easy (workflow).

### 3. Churn, and no win-back or attendance alerts
- **What happens:** members quietly stop coming and then cancel. Nobody notices the drop-off.
- **Who suffers:** owner MRR.
- **Cost:**
  - ~50% of new members gone in 6 months
  - 46% cancel because they aren't using it enough
  - 2x/week visitors are 50% less likely to cancel
  - (https://smarthealthclubs.com/blog/100-gym-membership-retention-statistics/ **[secondary]**)
- **Fix:**
  - an "absent 10 days" SMS or coach task
  - onboarding sequence for the first 90 days
  - win-back campaign to ex-members
  - **Ease:** medium (needs attendance data through the API or an export).

### 4. Failed payments / dunning
- **What happens:** cards expire and charges decline. The owner chases it by hand each week, or never does.
- **Who suffers:** owner or admin.
- **Cost:** 7–15% of charges fail. Involuntary churn is 30–40% of all churn **[UNVERIFIED vendor]**, and automation roughly doubles recovery.
- **Evidence:**
  - https://ustechautomations.com/resources/blog/automate-gym-billing-failure-recovery-workflow-2026
  - Glofox owner: "takes payments from customers banks on the wrong date"
- **Fix:**
  - SMS/email dunning with an update-card link
  - a voice call for high-value members
  - **Ease:** medium. Needs billing webhooks, and Stripe-based tools are easiest.

### 5. Phones and messages unanswered (plus unstaffed 24/7 hours), which leads to bad reviews
- **What happens:** calls go to voicemail or a full inbox, and DMs sit unread. Members turn to Google reviews.
- **Who suffers:** reputation, and new-lead conversion.
- **Evidence:** "does not accept voicemail, and more than five emails have gone unanswered" (https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.planetfitness.com)
- **Fix:**
  - AI voice receptionist (hours, pricing, freeze or cancel info, book a tour)
  - web chat
  - **Ease:** easy to medium.

### 6. Cancellation and billing friction (compliance plus reputation)
- **What happens:** in-person or letter-only cancels and surprise charges. This produces 1-star reviews, chargebacks and AG complaints.
- **Evidence:** the Planet Fitness quotes in section B.
- **Law:**
  - The FTC click-to-cancel rule was **vacated by the 8th Circuit on 8 July 2025** (https://www.sidley.com/en/insights/newsupdates/2025/07/us-ftc-click-to-cancel-rule-struck-down).
  - State auto-renewal laws (California, New York, etc.) still require easy cancellation, and the FTC is moving to revive the rule (https://www.crowell.com/en/insights/client-alerts/clicking-all-the-right-boxes-ftc-moves-to-revive-click-to-cancel-rule-following-eighth-circuit-vacatur).
- **Fix:**
  - online cancel/pause flow with a save offer (pause instead of cancel)
  - automated confirmation email
  - **Ease:** easy (website plus workflow).

### 7. Late cancels, no-shows and waitlists in classes
- **What happens:** 10–20% of booked spots go unused, and the waitlist isn't promoted in time.
- **Who suffers:** boutique owners, where a couple of empty bikes can wipe out a class's margin.
- **Evidence:**
  - Glofox blog, citing Mindbody (https://www.glofox.com/blog/fitness-class-cancellation-policy/)
  - one studio's late-cancel rate dropped from 18% to 9% after adding a $15 fee (same results set) **[UNVERIFIED]**
  - Glofox owner: the system "cant even send out an email for a cancelled class"
- **Fix:**
  - SMS reminders plus reply-to-cancel
  - text blast to the waitlist
  - class-cancelled SMS
  - **Ease:** medium (booking API).

### 8. Instructor scheduling and subs
- **What happens:** a sick instructor means a group-chat scramble at 6am.
- **Who suffers:** the owner in multi-instructor studios.
- **Evidence:** a dedicated app (ClassSub) exists to avoid "group-chat chaos" (https://classsub.inpulsd.com/)
- **Fix:**
  - sub-request SMS broadcast
  - first "yes" gets the class
  - members notified automatically
  - **Ease:** easy to medium.

### 9. Admin busywork (membership edits, review requests, referral asks)
- **Evidence:** "Amendments to memberships are cumbersome and require alot of manual input." (Glofox Capterra)
- **Fix:**
  - automated review request after milestones (10th class)
  - referral prompts
  - **Ease:** easy.

### By size
- **Solo studio:** pains 1, 2, 7 and 8. Wants cheap and simple, so chat plus reminders at roughly $150–300/mo.
- **Established gym:** pains 1–4 matter most, and it has the budget. This is the best target.
- **Multi-site:** pains 5 and 6 (call handling, cancellation compliance, reviews at scale). Needs integration with ABC/ClubReady and has a longer sales cycle.
