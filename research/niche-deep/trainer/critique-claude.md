# Claude's critique of gemini.md (trainers)

Short and harsh. Everything listed here is either fixed or dropped in REPORT.md.

## Unsourced or wrong stats
- **"Average no-show rate is 17-25%"**, sourced to noshowcalc.com. That's a generic calculator site covering all appointment businesses, not trainers. No sourced PT no-show rate exists (claude-voice.md looked and found none). Dropped from the report, and listed as a gap.
- **"SMS reminders cut no-shows by 80%"**: no source anywhere. Dropped.
- **"90% of traffic comes from Instagram bios"**: no source. Dropped. The direction is plausible for online coaches, but it's wrong for in-person PTs, where Google matters.
- **"GBP is the #1 lead source"**: no source. Kept as opinion for in-person trainers only.
- **"Trainers on Reddit say: 'You must enforce a 24-hour cancellation policy to survive.'"** The link goes to the subreddit homepage, so it isn't a quote from anyone. It looks invented. Dropped. The real 24h policy norm comes from coachway (vendor).
- **The fitsw.com quote** is used as evidence of *cost*, but it's a generic marketing sentence with no number in it.
- **"$1,200+/month lost"** is gemini's own math, stacked on the bad 17-25% figure. It's labelled (illustrative) in the report.

## Pricing errors
- **"Trainerize ($5-$250/mo)"** is wrong. The cheapest plan is ~$26.98 for 5 clients, and $164.98 for 50 (claude-market.md).
- **Vagaro and Mindbody** are studio and salon tools. Solo PTs mostly glue Calendly/Acuity, Stripe and a coaching app together. Gemini admitted this in its own critique.
- **The Premium tier's "AI voice agent"** solves a problem trainers don't have. Their leads come in by DM, not phone. The missed-call text-back in Core is weak for the same reason.
- **The package prices ($500/$1,000/$2,000 setup)** are OK for established trainers, but the same $297/mo cap on an online team that pays setters 2-5% of sales undercharges the one buyer with real money.

## Compliance errors
- **TCPA**: "a checkbox covers this" is too simple. Marketing texts need prior express written consent with clear disclosure. STOP must be honoured in any reasonable wording (rule from Apr 2025). US numbers need A2P 10DLC registration.
- **The Meta 24h messaging window** is missing entirely, and it's the #1 rule for the DM automation gemini is selling.
- **FTC** is missing entirely: before/after photos, "lose 10lbs in 90 days" copy (which gemini recommends!), fake or AI reviews (up to ~$53k per violation), and earnings claims for coaches who coach coaches.
- **The waiver and PAR-Q point** is good. Kept.

## What gemini got right (kept)
Split-shift rhythm, a DM-first lead flow, the "on the floor" auto-reply voice, pricing on the page, niche positioning, and card on file with auto-enforced cancellation fees.
