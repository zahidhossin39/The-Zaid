# Scoring rubric — ten prototypes

Two scores per prototype, each out of 10, one decimal place.

Target: **every prototype ≥ 8.5**. Anything below is revised and re-scored.

## Colour score (out of 10)

| Weight | Criterion |
|---|---|
| 30% | **Chromatic spine.** Do the neutrals carry the accent's temperature, so the accent looks native rather than applied? Pure greys score ≤ 5 regardless of anything else. |
| 25% | **Soothing over a long scroll.** Eye strain, halation, vibration against the ground. |
| 20% | **Trustworthy.** Would a stranger pay this person money? |
| 15% | **Bold / memorable.** Would it be remembered tomorrow? Generic scores low. |
| 10% | **Contrast compliance.** Accent ≥ 4.5:1 on its own bg; body text legible. |

## Design score (out of 10)

| Weight | Criterion |
|---|---|
| 30% | **Accent discipline.** Accent in 2–3 roles, never on headline type. Accent on the headline caps the design score at 6.0. |
| 25% | **Typographic quality.** Scale, line length, leading, no orphans, type matches the palette's register. |
| 25% | **Layout distinctiveness.** Genuinely different from the other nine, not a colour swap. |
| 20% | **Composition.** Balance, whitespace, hierarchy, where the eye lands first. |

## Hard rules — violating any caps the design score at 6.0

1. Accent colour used on the headline
2. Pure neutral greys with no hue
3. Accent in more than four roles
4. Layout substantially identical to another prototype

## Process

Claude, Kimi and Gemini each score independently, then argue. Where scores differ by more than 1.0, the disagreement is resolved explicitly and a single agreed number is recorded — not an average. Averaging hides the disagreement; the point is to find who is right.
