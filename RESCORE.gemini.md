# Rescore: Revised Prototypes

## 07 Aubergine Hour
**Colour: 8.7**
The rich magenta accent and deep purple-black spine are perfectly aligned. This creates a distinct and memorable palette that remains soothing over a long scroll.

**Design: 9.0**
The left-aligned vertical rail anchors the composition beautifully and eliminates the previous dead void. Resolving the floating panel into a full-width baseline band gives the layout the structural authority it lacked.

**Verdict:** PASS

## 08 Acid
**Colour: 8.8**
Rebuilding the neutrals with genuine chartreuse chroma completely fixes the spine issue. The loud acid green accent now feels native to the page rather than artificially applied.

**Design: 8.9**
The brutalist typography and heavy horizontal bands form a highly distinct and confident layout. Clearing the eyebrow from the corner label effortlessly resolves the previous spatial collision.

**Verdict:** PASS

## 09 One Note
**Colour: 8.7**
Introducing the pale ice blue accent alongside cool-tinted neutrals satisfies the chromatic spine rule beautifully. It rescues the prototype from being too monochrome while preserving its intended disciplined restraint.

**Design: 8.6**
The dead-centred column bounded by subtle hairlines provides excellent whitespace and focus. The tight Inter typography perfectly matches the quiet and serious register of the palette.

**Verdict:** PASS

## 10 Fog
**Colour: 8.6**
The periwinkle accent against deep blue-grey neutrals creates an incredibly trustworthy and soothing atmosphere. Allowing these colours to breathe directly on the page ground significantly improves their visual presence.

**Design: 8.8**
Stripping away the bordered modal wrapper was exactly the right call to fix the layout. The small-scale Newsreader typography now feels grounded and elegant rather than trapped in a cookie banner.

**Verdict:** PASS
