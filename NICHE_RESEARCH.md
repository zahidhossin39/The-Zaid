# Niches and problems: research and decision

Date: 2026-09-24. Services being sold: AI voice agents (answer and make calls, book jobs), AI chat/DM automation (Instagram, WhatsApp, Messenger, SMS, web chat) and conversion websites. Market: US first, also UK, Canada, Australia.

## How the research was run

- **Round 1:** a deep-research swarm (105 agents, 3-vote claim verification). Only 6 claims survived. Almost every popular statistic in this market was refuted as a vendor blog copying another vendor blog.
- **Round 2:** 7 targeted agents, each sent to primary sources only: Census, the original speed-to-lead studies, Invoca and CallRail's own data, LocalIQ ad benchmarks, HomeAdvisor cost guides, the Clio legal trends report, and a competitor count per niche.

## Statistics that must never appear on the site

All of these failed verification or could not be traced to an original study: "62% of calls go unanswered", "85% of callers never call back", "$126K lost per year to missed calls", per-industry revenue-per-missed-call figures, no-show rates by industry, "reminders cut no-shows 50%", "78% buy from the first to respond", "45% of calls come after hours", and AI adoption rates by industry.

## Scoring

Six criteria /10, weighted:
- **Pain** (20%): how much a lost lead costs
- **Pay** (20%): ability to afford a $1-5K setup plus a retainer
- **Fit** (20%): uses all three services
- **Open** (20%): how uncrowded the niche is, the inverse of saturation
- **Size** (10%): number of businesses
- **Speed** (10%): how quickly owners decide

These are judgment scores built on the evidence above, not measurements. Where data was missing, the score was kept conservative.

### Honest result on the 9.0 bar

**Only one niche clears 9.0.** It qualifies only after narrowing to med spas whose booking software has no built-in AI, which is backed by the competition research. The next six sit at 8.2-8.5. Each is held back by a real weakness, not by missing research:
- **Roofing, HVAC, dental and law** have the most valuable leads, but those markets are crowded. The software these owners already use now ships its own AI receptionist, or the market is flooded with specialist tools.
- **Auto repair, cleaning and pest control** are wide open, but each lead is worth less.

Pushing these to 9 would mean inventing numbers. The one thing a third round could legitimately move is the "Open" score for **roofing and remodelers**, by checking whether their software (JobNimbus, AccuLynx, Buildertrend) bundles AI yet. That was not verified this round.

### Recommended focus

1. **Beauty and wellness (med spas first, then salons).** Highest overall fit. Enquiries come in through Instagram DMs and calls, the customer value is high, and the booking tools they use have no AI receptionist.
2. **High-ticket home projects (remodelers, roofers).** One saved lead pays for the whole setup.
3. **Open trades (auto repair, cleaning, pest control).** Easiest to win as a new seller with no clients.

The site stays general, so all three groups see themselves in it, but examples and case studies should lead with these.

## The 5 problems, their fixes, and the outcome

Shared by most of the niches above, ordered by strength of evidence.

| # | The problem, in the owner's words | The fix | Outcome | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "I can't answer the phone while I'm working." | An AI voice agent answers every call, day or night, and books the job | No call goes unanswered | About a quarter of calls go unanswered, under 3% leave a voicemail, and 21% call a competitor straight away (Invoca, CallRail) |
| 2 | "By the time I reply, they've gone elsewhere." | Instant AI replies on Instagram, WhatsApp, Messenger, text and web chat that answer questions and book | Every enquiry answered in seconds | Replying within the hour gives about 7x better odds of qualifying the lead; 23% of firms never reply (HBR) |
| 3 | "People find me but don't book." | A fast website with booking built in, plus booking straight from chat | Visitors turn into booked appointments | 71-79% of beauty and med spa regulars have skipped a booking because it was hard to book (Zenoti); 17% of businesses have no website (Clutch) |
| 4 | "Happy customers never leave reviews." | An automatic review request after every job | A steady stream of fresh reviews | 47% avoid businesses with fewer than 20 reviews; 83% of people who were asked went on to review (BrightLocal) |
| 5 | "I can't hire enough people." | The system covers phones and inboxes without adding staff | You grow without adding headcount | NFIB about 35% unfilled openings (unverified); the weakest of the five |

### Using these in the hero video

- Show **3**: calls, messages and bookings. Reviews and hiring belong lower on the page.
- This does not break the "one message" rule. The three problems arrive from different channels (a missed call, an Instagram DM, a web form) and all settle into one calm state: **nothing waiting on you**. That single resolution is the message.
- **Use no statistics in the hero.** The numbers above are for the sections below it and for sales calls, each with its source named.
