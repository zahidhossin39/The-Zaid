# "Same afternoon, twice": story for the long film

**Use:** services section and social. **Not** the hero (the hero keeps the 8s calendar).
**Format:** 1920x1080, 30 fps, about 45 s, built to work muted. Code-built UI (HyperFrames), no faces, no people, never the word "AI".

## How the story was found
- Five isolated brainstorm agents, each with a different frame: game design, inversion, a 10-year-old, remove the load-bearing assumption, film director. They produced 30 ideas.
- Scored on novelty, viability and fit. Clustered, traps removed, top 3 deepened by 3 more agents. Channel and pacing rules came from a Gemini research pass.
- The 3 deepened spines:
  - **A. Replay:** the same 45-minute session twice, first lost, then caught.
  - **B. Face-down phone:** the owner is with a client while the phone lights up unseen.
  - **C. Client's-eye view:** she DMs Studio A, gets nothing, and books Studio B in seconds.
- Five of the 30 ideas independently invented "replay the same moment". That is the strongest signal in the set.

## Scoring rubric (weights)
Muted clarity 25 · Emotion/relatability 20 · Story craft 20 · Coverage of services and channels 10 · Doesn't feel like an ad 10 · Buildability and animation potential 15

## Iterations

### v1: spine C alone (client's-eye view) = 8.3
| Clarity | Emotion | Craft | Coverage | Not-ad | Build |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | 9 | 9 | 6 | 9 | 8 |

**Good:**
- Being ignored is felt from the side of the person who leaves.
- "sorry just seeing this!!" is a text every owner has sent.

**Bad:**
- The viewer is an owner but watches through a stranger's phone. They only learn "that's me" at the end, and anyone who scrolls away at 10 s gets no payoff.
- No phone call, which is dental's number-one channel.
- One portrait phone wastes a landscape frame.

### v2: A + B + C merged = 8.7
Two phones share one clock:
- On the left, **your phone** on the counter, lighting up while you're with a patient.
- On the right, **their phone**, showing what happens to the person you didn't answer.
- Act 1 loses three clients. Act 2 rewinds and replays the same afternoon, with every one caught.

| Clarity | Emotion | Craft | Coverage | Not-ad | Build |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | 9 | 9 | 8 | 8.5 | 8 |

**Bad:**
1. The rewind could read as a glitch or a loop.
2. Two phones split attention.
3. In Act 2, "who replied?" is unclear. If the owner looks like the one replying, the point is lost.
4. The owner asked for real information, and there is none.
5. Act 2 repeats Act 1 and could drag.

### v3: fixes = 9.2
| Fix | Solves |
|---|---|
| The rewind is a visible scrub: a bar and the clock running 2:45 back to 2:00, plus the caption "Same afternoon. Take two." | 1 |
| Spotlight rule: only one phone is lit at a time, the other dims, and the camera pushes to the active one | 2 |
| Act 2 opens with "You're still with a patient." Your phone stays on the counter, untouched, while replies land on theirs | 3 |
| One verified stat at the turning point, with its source: "1 in 5 callers who reach no one call a competitor straight away" (CallRail, 2025, n=1,000) | 4 |
| Act 2 runs faster, since the viewer already knows where to look, and ends on a new payoff: the lock-screen summary, then the week | 5 |
| The ending calls back to the hero: the week is full and Thursday evening is "Off" | craft |

| Clarity | Emotion | Craft | Coverage | Not-ad | Build | **Weighted** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9.5 | 9 | 9.5 | 8.5 | 9 | 8.5 | **9.15** |

**What still keeps it from 10:**
- Only 3 channels are shown in full. Messenger, WhatsApp and web chat appear only as summary lines.
- Win-back and reviews are left out.
- Both are deliberate: research says 3 to 5 ideas per 30 s. They belong in their own short follow-up cuts.

## Rules carried over
- Amber is the only accent: it marks an answered or booked moment and the "Off" evening.
- Lookalike app UIs use bubble colours and shapes only, with no Meta or Apple logos.
- No cross-fades. Entrances use expo.out and exits use power2.in. back.out is used exactly once, on the waitlist "YES".
- Sound: built silent. An optional sound bed (a buzz on the counter, ringing that cuts to calm, a soft chime on each booking) can be added for social later.
